## Retrieval

1. Define CAGR from the growth factor G.
2. State the drag formula and compute it for σ = 30% annual.
3. Which mean answers "what did I actually earn"? Which answers "what should I expect next period"?
4. Annualize: (a) a 0.02% mean daily simple return, (b) a 1.1% daily vol.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. $CAGR = G^{1/\text{years}} - 1$ with $G = \prod_t(1+r_t)$.
2. $\bar r_{arith} - \bar r_{geo} \approx \sigma^2/2$ = 0.09/2 = 4.5% per year — huge.
3. Geometric (CAGR) for what you earned; arithmetic for the expected next-period return.
4. (a) compound: $1.0002^{252}-1 \approx 5.2\%$; (b) $1.1\%\times\sqrt{252}\approx17.5\%$.

</details>
