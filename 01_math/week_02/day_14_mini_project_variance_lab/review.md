<details><summary>Notes</summary>

Typical real-data findings: the curve flattens by n ≈ 10–20 toward the
average-covariance floor; average covariance roughly doubles in high-vol
regimes (so a calm-sized portfolio is ~40% riskier than designed when the
storm arrives); estimation error usually eats most of the perfect-information
gap — your Part-3 A-weights often underperform 1/N, which is DeMiguel et
al.'s (2009) entire point, arriving formally in module 11.
