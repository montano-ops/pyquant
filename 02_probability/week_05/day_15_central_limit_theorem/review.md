# Review — Central Limit Theorem

## Retrieval

1. State the CLT; the two-distributions distinction.
2. The three strain-cases in finance.
3. Why is "returns aren't normal so t-tests are invalid" the wrong worry?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Standardized sample mean → N(0,1) under finite variance + independence;
   parent (fat, skewed) vs sampling distribution of the mean (≈normal,
   narrow).
2. Fat tails (slow convergence, bad tails); dependence (n_eff < n);
   nonstationarity (mixture, no single μ).
3. t-tests need the *mean's* sampling distribution, which CLT supplies;
   the right worry is n, dependence, and what the test is applied to.

</details>
