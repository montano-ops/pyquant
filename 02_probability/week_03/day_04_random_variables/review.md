# Review — Random Variables & Distributions

## Retrieval

1. PMF vs PDF vs CDF — which answers a percentile question?
2. Why is P(X = x) = 0 for continuous X?
3. What does calling a distribution a "model" commit you to?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. CDF: F(x)=P(X≤x); invert it for quantiles/VaR.
2. A point has zero width; probability is area under the density.
3. A claim about the data-generating process (shape, tails, stability) that
   can be wrong and should be tested — not a fact about the data.

</details>
