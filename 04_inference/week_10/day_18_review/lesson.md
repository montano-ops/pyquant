## The module's spine (if you can say this, module 04 worked)

> Inference is the reasoning from a sample to a claim, and every
> claim arrives with three addresses: an **estimate** (what size), an
> **SE** (under what design), and a **family** (how many claims were
> in play). The resampling tools (bootstrap, permutation, blocks)
> build the distributions the formulas assume. The grill audits the
> first two axes of a claim (real? how real?); the gate audits the
> second axis of the *trade* (worth owning?); the cost gate says
> "statistically real" and "economically alive" are different
> sentences, and both must be true.
