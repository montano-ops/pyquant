# Day 18 Review — Week 10 Review

## Retrieval (answers)

1. Grill order: effect size (money, CI) → power (detectable = 2.8·SE)
   → multiplicity (family, thresholds, the zoo) → design honesty
   (overlap/pairing/clustering). The t is the last thing you trust.
2. SE = coef/t; band/effect = 1.96/|t|; annualize by frequency.
3. Detectable = 2.8·SE (80% power, two-sided, large-n).
4. Checklist (six of ten): unit of observation; null; design; SE
   flavor; sample; family; money units; SE matches design?;
   economic story; what would kill it.
5. The table is its own family; the exemplar: 4 stars → 1 survivor
   (Bonferroni), 3 under BH (FDR game).
6. Gate inputs: gross, vol, events/yr, cost (at a stated size).
   Outputs: net Sharpe grid; death cost.
7. Death cost = gross_bp/events per event; × events = the annual
   cost line.
8. Event-frequency trap: same annual gross, more events → smaller
   per-event gross, same per-event cost → different strategy,
   different verdict.
9. Reversal: ~100bp/week gross, 52 events, 10bp kill-switch.
10. Spine: axis 1 real-or-not (grill); axis 2 worth-owning (gate);
    both must clear.

## Module 04's spine (say it in full)

> Inference reasons from sample to claim; every claim carries an
> estimate, an SE (under a design), and a family (a count). The
> resampling tools build the distributions the formulas assume. The
> grill audits the claim; the gate audits the trade. "Statistically
> real" and "economically alive" are different sentences.
