## Retrieval (answers)

1. P1: the first line of a paired audit IS the design (matched
   dates, shared market); sd(diff) from the covariance term; the
   pairing dividend at ρ = 0.72 ≈ 1.5×.
2. P2: bootstrap SE ≈ textbook SE (the unit test); clustered SE
   ≈ ×√(n/n_eff); the spread measures the destroyed clustering.
3. P3: permutation ≈ Fisher when the data are near-iid; wrong on
   autocorrelated/cluttered series — check the ACF first.
4. P4: noise at 80 candidates: ~3.6 at |t|>2; BH "survivors" in a
   pure-noise screen are the expected false discoveries — the
   procedure counts them, it doesn't certify them.
5. P5: naive 9-t → corrected 2-t (√21); non-overlapping N/21 gives
   the same 2-t: the designs agree, the overlap never created
   information.
6. P6: the audit paragraph = design → honest number → family →
   cost → conditional verdict.
