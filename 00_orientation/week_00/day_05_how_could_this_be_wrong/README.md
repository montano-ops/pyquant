# Day 5 — How Could This Be Wrong?

**Module 00 · Week 0 · ~90–120 min**

**Objective:** meet the six biases that ruin backtests, and *manufacture* a
Sharpe-1.5 strategy from pure noise with your own hands. Today's exercise is
the emotional anchor of the entire course.

**Prerequisites:** day 4.

**Files:** lesson.md → exercise.ipynb → solution.ipynb → review.md

**Research connection:** the entire "research validation" literature you will
meet properly in module 13 (Harvey, Liu & Zhu; Bailey & López de Prado). Today
you learn its lesson experimentally, before the formulas.

**Self-check:** a backtest shows Sharpe 1.4 over 10 years. List four distinct
questions you should ask before believing a dollar of it.
