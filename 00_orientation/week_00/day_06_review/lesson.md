# Day 6 — Review: Orientation

Review days are retrieval days. Read nothing first. Work `review.md` cold,
from memory, writing answers down. Then (and only then) check the `<details>`
blocks, reread the corresponding lesson sections for anything you missed, and
update your error log and spaced-repetition schedule.

Today you also set up your permanent systems:

1. **PROGRESS.md** — mark days 1–5 done with honest minutes; write your
   one-line-per-day study log entries (retroactively is fine, today only).
2. **Spaced-repetition schedule** — copy the first rows from the template
   (log vs simple returns, adjusted close, the six biases, JT93's data spec)
   and schedule them at +1 week, +1 month.
3. **Error log** — your first entries: anything this week that surprised you,
   any diagnostic task you got wrong, any exercise where the solution taught
   you something you thought you knew.

The measure of a review day is not "feels familiar" — it's "can produce on a
blank page". Next week (module 01) opens with warm-up retrieval from *this*
week. Be ready.
