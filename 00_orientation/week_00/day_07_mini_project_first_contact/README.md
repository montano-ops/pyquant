# Day 7 — Mini-Project: First Contact

**Module 00 · Week 0 · ~120–150 min**

**Objective:** a complete, if tiny, research cycle on three assets: acquire
data, compute returns and risk, answer five research questions, and write a
one-page data diary with a bias audit.

**Prerequisites:** days 1–6.

**Files:** lesson.md (the brief) → exercise.ipynb (your workspace) →
solution.ipynb (a worked exemplar — compare, don't copy) → review.md

**Deliverables:** a one-page write-up answering Q1–Q5, plus a reflection
section. This is the first entry in your research log.

**Self-check:** could you explain to a friend why your conclusions might be
wrong? (If not, you're not done.)
