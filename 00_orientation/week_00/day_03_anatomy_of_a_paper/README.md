# Day 3 — Anatomy of a Research Paper

**Module 00 · Week 0 · ~90–120 min**

**Objective:** learn the 12-step paper workflow and apply steps 1–4 to a real
momentum paper — with zero statistics. Reading papers is a skill you build
from day 3, not a reward at the end.

**Prerequisites:** day 1. (Day 2 helpful but not required.)

**Files:** lesson.md → exercise.ipynb → solution.ipynb → review.md

**Research connection:** the paper itself — Jegadeesh & Titman (1993),
*Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers*, Journal of Finance. You read
it today; you reproduce it in module 07; you extend it in capstone 1.

**Self-check:** can you state, from memory, what data JT93 used, what they
sorted stocks on, and what the headline finding was (approximately)?
