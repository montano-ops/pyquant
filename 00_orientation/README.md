# Module 00 — Orientation (Week 0)

Seven days that make every later day possible:

1. How the course works — the method, the rules of engagement, your environment.
2. Your toolkit, tested — a pandas diagnostic on *financial* data (this course assumes your pandas and tests it where research needs it).
3. Anatomy of a research paper — the 12-step workflow, applied to a real paper (read-only; no statistics required yet).
4. Prices and returns — your first data investigation: close vs adjusted close, simple vs log returns.
5. How could this be wrong? — the tour of biases, and a visceral demonstration: you will manufacture a fantastic backtest from pure noise.
6. Review — retrieval, your tracker, your spaced-repetition system.
7. Mini-project: first contact — a small end-to-end study of three assets, written up like a researcher.

**Learning objectives.** By the end of this module you can:

- run every course notebook from a working environment;
- describe the daily/weekly rhythm and the rules of engagement;
- dissect a paper into question / intuition / hypothesis / data / methodology;
- explain the difference between prices, adjusted prices, simple returns, log returns — and when each is correct;
- name the six biases that will haunt every backtest, with a one-line example of each;
- demonstrate to yourself, from simulation, why a great backtest proves nothing by itself.

**Papers this module:** Jegadeesh & Titman (1993) — *read only* (steps 1–4 of the paper workflow). You will reproduce it in module 07.
